PROFESSIONAL REFLECTION

What I have learned

Reflecting on my development as an aspiring IT professional.

During this module, I have learned that having a professional digital presence is an important part of developing as an IT professional. I learned how to plan and structure a professional portfolio website and how different elements such as navigation, responsive design, images, hyperlinks and page layouts contribute to the overall user experience. I also developed a better understanding of basic SEO principles and the importance of making a website accessible and easy to navigate on different devices.

The IT professional skill that I currently consider one of my strongest is problem-solving. Programming has taught me to approach problems logically, break them into smaller parts and work towards a solution. I have also developed my programming knowledge through Java and gained practical experience with web technologies such as HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Working on projects has helped me become more comfortable with applying what I learn rather than only studying the theory.

A skill I would like to improve is my programming ability, particularly when working with more complex JavaScript and larger applications. I sometimes need more practice to understand how different parts of a program work together. To improve, I plan to practise programming regularly, complete additional coding exercises and build more personal projects. I will also make use of documentation, tutorials and feedback when I encounter concepts that I find difficult.

This portfolio can support my future studies and career by providing a professional place where I can record my progress and showcase the projects I complete. As I continue through my Computer Science studies, I can add new projects, technical skills, certifications and other achievements. It can eventually become a useful resource when applying for internships or employment because it will show how my skills have developed over time. Keeping the portfolio updated will also encourage me to continue learning and building practical experience.
